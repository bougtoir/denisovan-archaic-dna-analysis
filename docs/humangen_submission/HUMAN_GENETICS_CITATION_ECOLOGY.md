## Novelty boundary (explicit)

No prior study fits a structural null to pairwise archaic-segment similarity profiles across human populations and asks whether any pair exceeds it. The closest works either diagnose structure bias in fitted models (Mazet; Tournebize & Chikhi; Ortega-Del Vecchyo & Slatkin; Lawson) or supply the permutation machinery (Mantel; Krackhardt; Dekker). Our contribution is the inferential framing + workflow + first application to human archaic-ancestry profiles — honestly bounded in the text.
