# EDITORIAL_DESK_REVIEW — Human Genetics desk-reject assessment

## Answers to the 15 editorial questions

1. **Unmistakably human genetics?** YES. All data are human (3,134 individuals, 66 populations, 1000G+HGDP); the application is archaic introgression in human populations; the motivation is human pairwise-comparison inference.
2. **Biological problem important enough?** YES — how to calibrate "this pair is exceptional" claims is a recurring human population-genetics problem with real overclaiming history (ABO narratives cited).
3. **Beyond descriptive population genetics?** YES — it supplies an inferential workflow (structural null + pair-level residual test + FDR), not a population survey.
4. **Inferential contribution materially improves interpretation?** YES, modestly — it operationalises a known dependence problem into a prespecified test; improvement is methodological-behavioral rather than a new estimator.
5. **Novelty clear in title/abstract/first 2 pages/Figure 1/cover letter?** YES — title names the structural null; abstract states non-independence + the test; Intro ¶3–4 defines it; Figure 1(e) diagrams the workflow; cover letter ¶3 states it within ~150 words.
6. **Too methodological?** NO — methods serve a concrete human-data question; no theorem-style content.
7. **Too descriptive?** PARTIAL RISK — the result is a null; mitigated by framing as a calibrated-expectation demonstration (see #9–10).
8. **Archaic introgression central enough?** YES — flagship human evolutionary-genetics topic; ABO worked example is recognisable to the readership.
9. **Negative result reduces priority?** MODERATE residual risk — the classic desk-reject vulnerability; manuscript mitigates by making the calibrated null itself the finding (Discussion ¶1) and the cover letter argues it disciplines future claims.
10. **Why is the null useful?** Answered explicitly: nominal outliers (|z| up to 2.53) exist but vanish under the correct expectation — prevents false pair-specific narratives.
11. **Closest prior studies acknowledged prominently?** YES — Mazet 2016, Tournebize & Chikhi 2025, Ortega-Del Vecchyo & Slatkin 2019, Lawson et al. 2020, Mantel/QAP literature all in Intro/Discussion ¶1–2.
12. **"Better suited to a methods journal"?** Possible but weakened — contribution is a workflow/framing, not a new method; human data + human inference keep it in scope.
13. **"Insufficient human-genetics advance"?** Possible — the empirical yield is a null; countered by the worked-example discipline argument.
14. **"Descriptive population structure already known"?** Preempted — Discussion ¶2 explicitly disclaims the novelty of structure-bias and locates novelty in the testable workflow.
15. **Proactive answers?** YES for all three objections.
