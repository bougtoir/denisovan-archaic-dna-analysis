# Human Genetics requirements audit

Source: https://link.springer.com/journal/439/submission-guidelines (Submission guidelines, accessed 2026-10-14) and https://link.springer.com/journal/439/aims-and-scope, https://link.springer.com/journal/439/how-to-publish-with-us (fees). Springer Nature shared policies apply.

## Journal fit

- Scope (verbatim, paraphrased): Human Genetics is a monthly journal publishing original and timely articles on all aspects of human genetics, including gene structure and organization, gene expression, mutation detection and analysis, linkage analysis and genetic mapping, physical mapping, cytogenetics and genomic imaging, genome structure and organization, disease association studies, molecular diagnostics, genetic epidemiology, evolutionary genetics, developmental genetics, genotype–phenotype relationships, molecular genetics of tumorigenesis, genetics of complex diseases and epistatic interactions, ethical/legal/social issues, and bioinformatics. Animal articles relevant to human biology are also considered. Preference is given to articles of relevance to human biology.
- Fit assessment: the manuscript's "evolutionary genetics" and population-genetics framing is in scope; the dyadic-permutation methodology is a general contribution that must be presented through its human population-genetic application (retarget does exactly this). No clinical/disease claims are made — appropriate.
- Editors-in-Chief (verified 2026-10-14): Claudia Gonzaga-Jauregui and Maggie H. Wang. Cover letter addressed "Dear Editors-in-Chief,".

## Article type

- Research Article — original research article; the standard type for this manuscript.
- Springer does not impose a fixed page/word limit for Research Articles; concision is expected. No imposed figure/table count limit found in the guidelines.

## Required structure and format

- Abstract: 150–250 words, unstructured (no headings/citations/reference to undefined abbreviations). Our abstract is within this window (verified by validator).
- Keywords: 4–6 keywords for indexing. Manuscript supplies 6: population structure; archaic introgression; dyadic data; permutation tests; Neanderthals; Denisovans.
- Heading levels: no more than three levels displayed. Our structure uses Introduction / Materials and Methods (with level-2 subsections) / Results (level-2) / Discussion — compliant.
- Manuscript file: editable format required — .docx submitted (Word). LaTeX not used.
- Title page must include: title, author names, affiliations, corresponding author email, Abstract, Keywords, Acknowledgments (incl. funding and grants). Our title page includes title, author, affiliation, ORCID, corresponding email, abstract, keywords, acknowledgements, and declarations block.
- Declarations entered via the submission interface: Funding, Competing Interests, Ethics approval, Consent, Data availability, Materials availability, Code availability, Author contribution. The same declarations are stated in the manuscript body (Funding, Author Contributions, Competing Interests, Data Availability, Ethics Statement).
- Figures: submitted as separate files (not embedded in the manuscript text); figure legends listed at the end of the manuscript. Provided as PNG + TIFF in figures/ plus a PPTX compendium.
- Tables: numbered, cited in text in consecutive order; our main table (Table 1) and Table S1 are separate docx/xlsx files.
- References: Springer reference style — author–date citation in text (e.g. Smith and Jones 2020; Smith et al. 2020), reference list alphabetical by first author; format `Author AB, Author CD (year) Article title. Journal Name Volume:pages. https://doi.org/...` — all 31 entries converted to this format with full DOI links. Reference list checked alphabetically by validator.

## Policy items

- Peer review: single-anonymized (standard Springer Nature default).
- Generative AI: LLMs cannot be authors; AI use must be documented in the Methods or a suitable part of the manuscript. Copy-editing-only AI use need not be declared. Our "Declaration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Scientific Writing" section is retained (Devin used for formatting/wording/coding assistance) — more conservative than required, kept per prior established text.
- Ethics: manuscript must include ethics statements for human data; our Ethics Statement documents secondary analysis of de-identified public data, source-study approvals, no re-identification or community harm, CARE-aligned interpretation, no new phenotype inference. Retained unchanged.
- Data availability statement: optional but recommended; ours names Zenodo (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14136628), Dryad (doi.org/10.5061/dryad.zw3r228gg), and the public GitHub analysis code — all live links verified previously.
- Preprints: allowed (Springer Nature policy); manuscript was previously posted to bioRxiv — declare at submission.
- Competing interests: none declared. Funding: none.
- ORCID: corresponding author ORCID included on title page.

## Charges / publishing route

- Human Genetics is a hybrid journal: the traditional (subscription) route carries NO mandatory charge; Open Choice (open access) is optional (APC applies only if chosen). Fits the zero-mandatory-fee constraint.
- Color: no charge for color in the online version (standard Springer policy); print color optional/paid — online color sufficient.
