# Molecular Ecology submission checklist

## Journal parameters (verified against current Wiley author guidelines, 2026-10-19)

- Journal: Molecular Ecology (Wiley)
- Article type: Original Article
- Word limit: 8,000 words (Abstract + Introduction + Materials and Methods + Results + Discussion)
- Abstract: ~250 words
- Keywords: 4-6
- Title: major keywords within first 65 characters; running title <45 characters
- Free Format submission: any consistent style; editable file; figures/tables may be embedded or separate; SI in separate files
- Reference style: Wiley author-date (consistent author-year applied throughout)
- Manuscript order: Title Page, Abstract, Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Acknowledgements, References, Data Accessibility and Benefit-Sharing, Author Contributions, Tables and Figures with captions
- Data archiving: data supporting results must be publicly archived at acceptance (Dryad preferred - journal pays fee); all data/code must be available for review at submission; Private-for-Peer-Review encouraged; FAIR + CARE principles
- Cover letter must state fit with Aims and Scope
- Charges: traditional subscription route $0; no page charges for Original Articles; optional open access (OnlineOpen) APC ~US$5,210; colour print charges may apply (free online)
- Peer review: single-anonymous (Wiley default)
- AI disclosure: truthful declaration included
- ORCID iD required at submission
- Portal: https://authors.wiley.com/journal/MEC

- Confirm correspondence details, funding, conflict-of-interest statement, and ORCID.
- Confirm the data-archive route (Dryad; journal pays archiving fee) and Data Accessibility and Benefit-Sharing statement wording.
- Upload supporting information as separate files.
- Cover letter includes the required Aims-and-Scope fit statement.
- Do not interpret nominal residuals or the two Indigenous-American ABO-window segments as migration evidence.
